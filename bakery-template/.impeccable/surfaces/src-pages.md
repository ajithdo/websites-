# Surface brief: bakery site (all public pages)

Scope: every public page of the demo/template. Visitor mode: **Persuade** for Home/About/Gallery; **Operate** for the Menu cart and Cake builder (task first, brand in the details).

Audience & job: a Hanamkonda bakery owner judging the demo on a phone in 60 seconds; after a sale, local customers ordering over WhatsApp.
Action: design a cake or add bakes, then send a ready-written WhatsApp order.
Proof: the bakes themselves (photography), the working builder/cart, and the order message the site writes.
Constraints: pinned palettes and font pairings (3 themes), pinned section order, honest-demo labels, Lighthouse targets, no backend.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A patisserie's order book set as an editorial magazine: every section sells by showing the bake and ends in a WhatsApp action. It refuses the local template default (banner slider, identical card grid, contact form).

OWN-WORLD: Brief-pinned. Classic: cream paper ground; blush and espresso fields own whole regions; gold is hairline, seal and ribbon, never body text (gold-ink shade for text). Fraunces at optical display sizes with one italic accent word; Manrope for UI. Arch-topped photo frames, tilted overlapping prints, paper grain, patisserie ribbon rules. Signature component: the order ticket, a receipt-like card that live-previews the exact WhatsApp message. Pastel and Cocoa re-skin the same grammar.

STORY: See irresistible, specific bakes → believe this is a careful family patisserie → design a cake or add items → tap once to send a finished WhatsApp order.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Mobile 375: full-bleed Ken Burns photo at ~88svh, bottom-aligned: descriptor line, 44–48px Fraunces headline with italic accent, one-line subhead, primary "Design your cake" + ghost "Explore the menu", trust row; slim header over the photo with wordmark and WhatsApp. Desktop: same, text left, plus an overlapping tilted "at the counter today" print on the right.

FORM: Brief-pinned editorial patisserie; concept roll not run because the brief pins palette, fonts, structure and mood (brief-pinned direction beats the roll). Signature interaction: the order ticket composing itself in the builder and cart.

FINISH: done only after batched screenshot QA at 375/768/1280/1440 in all three themes, one critique + one fix round, detector run, Lighthouse on key pages, and every photo credited in CREDITS.md.
